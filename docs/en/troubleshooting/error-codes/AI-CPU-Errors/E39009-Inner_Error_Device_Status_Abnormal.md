# E39009 Inner\_Error\_Device\_Status\_Abnormal

## Symptom

```text
Failed to connect the device.
```

## Possible Cause

1. Heartbeats are missing on the device.
2. The underlying channel is disconnected.

## Solution

1. Obtain the host and device logs, and contact technical support at https://www.hiascend.com/support.
2. Restart the device.
